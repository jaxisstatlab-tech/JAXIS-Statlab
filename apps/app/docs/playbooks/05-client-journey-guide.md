## 2. Step-by-Step Client Flow

### Step 1: Submitting Your Research Study
1. Navigate to `/dashboard/client/submit`.
2. Fill out your study title, research objective, and methodology requirements (e.g., ANOVA, Multiple Linear Regression, Factor Analysis).
3. Select your desired delivery turnaround:
   - **Standard**: 7–10 Business Days
   - **Rush**: 3–5 Business Days (+25% fee)
   - **Emergency**: 24–48 Hours (+50% fee)
4. Upload your dataset file (`.xlsx`, `.csv`, `.sav`) and click **"Submit for Evaluation"**.

### Step 2: Receiving the Formal Quotation & Signing SOW
1. Within 24 hours, JAXIS Admin sends an itemized quotation to `/dashboard/client/quotations`.
2. Review the breakdown:
   - Statistical Analysis & Model Computation
   - APA 7th Interpretation Report & Data Visualization Charts
   - Optional: DefenseLab Oral Mock Defense Simulation
3. Click **"Review & Sign Contract"** to digitally sign the legal Statement of Work (SOW).

### Step 3: Depositing the 50% Escrow Downpayment
1. Select your preferred payment method (**GCash** or **BDO/BPI Corporate Bank Transfer**).
2. Scan the official QR code or copy the account details provided on screen.
3. Upload your payment screenshot and enter the transaction reference number.
4. Once Finance verifies the deposit, the funds are safely locked in escrow, and your assigned Lead Statistician starts analyzing your data immediately.

### Step 4: Reviewing Drafts & Receiving Final Deliverables
1. When analysis is complete and verified by the Senior QA Lead, you receive a notification.
2. Review the initial watermarked draft tables and interpretations.
3. Pay the remaining 50% final milestone balance.
4. Once cleared, click **"Download Verified Deliverables"** to receive:
   - Official APA 7th Formatted Summary Document (.docx / .pdf)
   - Full Statistical Syntax & Code Scripts (.R / .sps)
   - High-Resolution Publication Charts & Correlation Matrices (.png / .svg)
   - Official JAXIS QA Verification Certificate
