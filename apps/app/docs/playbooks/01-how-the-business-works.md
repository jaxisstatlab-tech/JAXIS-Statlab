## 1. Overview

JAXIS StatLab operates like an elite scientific laboratory and academic consultation firm. We help graduate students, institutional researchers, and corporate clients analyze complex data, interpret statistical models, and prepare verified research papers compliant with APA 7th standards.

---

## 2. The 7-Stage Project Lifecycle

Every project follows a strict 7-stage workflow from first contact to final archival:

```
STAGE 1: INTAKE & TRIAGE
────────────────────────
Client fills out submission form at /dashboard/client/submit
• Uploads raw dataset (.csv, .sav, .xlsx)
• Defines hypothesis and research questions
• Selects turnaround speed: Standard (7-10d), Rush (3-5d), Emergency (24-48h)
        │
        ▼
STAGE 2: QUOTATION & PRICING
────────────────────────────
Admin reviews data complexity and generates a formal quote:
• Base package (e.g. Core Statistical Analysis: ₱12,000)
• Add-ons (e.g. Oral Defense Rehearsal / DefenseLab: +₱3,500)
• Downpayment requirement: 50%
        │
        ▼
STAGE 3: SOW CONTRACT & 50% ESCROW DEPOSIT
──────────────────────────────────────────
• Client reviews and digitally signs the Statement of Work (SOW)
• Client transfers 50% deposit via GCash or BDO/BPI Bank Transfer
• Finance Officer verifies payment proof ────► Escrow locked in JAXIS Vault
        │
        ▼
STAGE 4: EXPERT ASSIGNMENT & TIME TRACKING
──────────────────────────────────────────
• Operations Admin assigns a qualified Lead Statistician
• Specialist clocks in via topbar and begins data hygiene & model runs
• Specialist and Client communicate exclusively through the secure platform
        │
        ▼
STAGE 5: SENIOR QA REVIEW & REPRODUCIBILITY AUDIT
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Statistician uploads APA summary tables and R/SPSS syntax scripts
• Senior QA Lead reviews calculations, formulas, and hypothesis tests
• If issues exist: QA requests revisions
• If verified: QA approves and stamps official clearance
        │
        ▼
STAGE 6: FINAL SETTLEMENT & DELIVERABLE RELEASE
──────────────────────────────────────────────
• Client inspects watermarked review draft
• Client transfers remaining 50% final balance
• Finance clears deposit ────► Final unwatermarked report & datasets unlocked
        │
        ▼
STAGE 7: PAYDAY DISBURSEMENT (15th / 30th)
──────────────────────────────────────────
• Payroll engine calculates specialist's study commission and duty hours
• Finance clicks 1-Click Copy and releases payment to staff GCash/Bank account
• Official statement voucher generated for specialist's historical ledger
```

## 3. Why the Escrow Vault Matters

In freelance data analysis, two common risks exist:
1. **Client Risk**: Paying 100% upfront to an unknown person who delivers incorrect math.
2. **Specialist Risk**: Spending 40 hours building statistical models only for the client to disappear without paying.

**The JAXIS Solution**:
- JAXIS acts as the trusted escrow agent.
- Client funds are locked safely in escrow before work begins.
- Specialists are guaranteed payment upon QA signoff.
- Clients are protected because funds are only released after scientific verification.
